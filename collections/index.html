The collections

Maison means home, and a house has many rooms. Sirivan designs what you wear and what you live with — and we choose a few things from friends whose work we admire.

Made in our workshop

  • Ready-to-wear for women and men — easy, well-cut pieces in linen, cotton and rayon, comfortable in the heat and elegant anywhere. Every model in every size.
  • Loungewear — kimonos, bathrobes and pyjamas.
  • Accessories — hats and leather sandals.
  • Home textiles — cushion covers, kapok mattresses, table linen.

Most fabrics are remnants from Cambodia's garment factories — available once, impossible to find again — so each model exists in a short run only.

Every piece is designed by Sirivan and made by our team at Sirivan Atelier de Couture in Siem Reap.
