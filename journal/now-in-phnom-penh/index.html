Now in Phnom Penh, at Atelier 464

Since March 2026, Maison Sirivan has a second home in Phnom Penh: Atelier 464, a shared space for contemporary Cambodian design in Tuol Tom Poung.

Four designers from Siem Reap had the same idea: show the capital what is being made in Cambodia today, in one place. Atelier 464 brings together fashion, art, footwear and furniture, and Maison Sirivan is one of its founding brands.

You will find a selection of our ready-to-wear for women and men, our hand-woven scarves and a few home pieces — all designed by Sirivan and made in our workshop in Siem Reap.

Where

Atelier 464, 30B Street 464, Tuol Tom Poung, Phnom Penh. All our addresses.
